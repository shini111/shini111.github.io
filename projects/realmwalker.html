The Build

An augmented reality combat RPG for Android, built for an AR application development course. Real-world walking (tracked through the device's step counter) triggers battles, and enemies spawn directly onto real surfaces through marker-less plane detection, no printed markers or QR codes required. On top of that sits a full RPG progression loop: a gear system with four rarity tiers, region-exclusive legendary drops tied to real-world location, and standard leveling.

The whole thing runs without requiring an internet connection or GPS for core combat, though the region-based gear system does use location and timezone data when available.

What I Did

  • Sole game programmer on the project, covering the AR integration, combat systems, and the full gear and progression loop
  • Implemented tap-and-swipe combat handling up to three simultaneous enemy types
  • Built the plane-detection and enemy-spawn pipeline on top of AR Foundation and ARCore
