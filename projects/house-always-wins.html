The Build

Built for the StayLab Masters Game Jam, a jam specifically for Master's students, in 48 hours with team Rigged Deal Studio. The core tension is between two systems working against each other: you can rig the wheel to control outcomes, but every rig raises a suspicion meter. Push it too far and the table gets shut down. The whole game is a management problem wearing a noir aesthetic.

What I Did

  • Programmed core systems and sound as one of two programmers on the team
  • Built a full finite state machine to drive game flow and table states
  • Used ScriptableObject-driven mechanics to keep the rigging and suspicion systems tunable without touching code, useful under jam time pressure when balance changes every hour
