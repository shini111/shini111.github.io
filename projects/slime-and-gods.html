The Build

The final project for my Bachelor's in Digital Games and Multimedia. A deck-building card game built around collecting and combining hero slimes to take down boss gods based on real mythology, each with their own designed kit and card mechanics. Built with a four-person team over several months as the closing project of the degree.

What I Did

  • Programmed as one of two programmers on the team
  • Worked through a full project cycle, from prototype to a finished, released build, as part of a formal capstone requirement
